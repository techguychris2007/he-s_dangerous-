## Other machine learning resources

The library stores descriptive catalog entries and links to the official author,
publisher or project. Their books, documentation, notebooks and datasets are not
mirrored. See each entry's source and usage terms. An open-source notebook license
does not automatically apply to its companion textbook, included datasets or models.

Offline saving in DarkWorld applies only to titles with an in-library PDF. External
resources require a connection; their own sites may offer separate downloads.
