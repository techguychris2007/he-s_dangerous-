DarkWorld Academy Refund Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026. This version is being finalised before paid plans launch.

Overview

This policy explains when you can get your money back for a DarkWorld Pro or Max plan. It forms part of the Terms of Service. Contact techguychris2007@gmail.com for any refund request, and include the payment reference shown on your Billing page or in Paystack's receipt email.

Always refunded

We refund the full amount when:

  • you were charged twice for the same purchase;
  • you were charged but your plan was not applied, and we cannot fix it within 3 working days of your report;
  • a renewal was charged after you had cancelled the subscription;
  • a technical fault on DarkWorld's side stopped you using the paid content for most of the period you paid for.

Report these within 14 days of the charge.

First purchase

If DarkWorld Pro or Max is not right for you, ask for a refund within 7 days of your first payment for that plan. We refund the full amount and end the paid access. This applies once per account.

Renewals and passes

After the first 7 days, payments are not refunded for unused time, except as listed under "Always refunded" or where the law requires it. You can cancel a card subscription at any time on the Billing page; renewal stops and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Mobile money and card passes do not renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back through Paystack to the card or mobile money wallet that made the payment. We start the refund within 5 working days of approving it. How long it then takes to reach you depends on your bank or mobile money provider. When a refund is issued, the paid access it covered ends.

Misuse

We may refuse a refund when an account has broken the Terms of Service, for example by sharing paid access or copying course content, or when refund requests are being abused. This does not affect a right you have under consumer protection law.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will show its update date. A change does not apply to a payment made before it took effect.
